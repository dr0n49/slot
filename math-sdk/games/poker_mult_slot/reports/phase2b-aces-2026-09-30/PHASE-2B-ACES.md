# Queen A deck — four ace approval group

The twelve courts were approved by the user after correcting the extra neck bones on K♥ and K♦. These four aces reuse Queen A's approved card material and common frame. Every A, suit, mirrored index, engraved hatch and ornamental stroke is authored vector geometry. The paper is the only raster layer.

Review `aces-desktop-native.png` at 86×121 and `aces-portrait-native.png` at 60×85, or open `native-size-proof.html` at 100% zoom. Sources are in `vectors/`; 512×720 masters and exact native-size exports are in `previews/`. `asset-manifest.json` records reread sizes and hashes. Run `node reports/phase2b-aces-2026-09-30/build-previews.cjs` from the game directory to reproduce them using the bundled image renderer.

The user's first revision removed the oval enclosure around the central suit on all four aces, including its surrounding radial ticks. The earlier native-size proofs are retained in `revisions/with-oval/`. The current vector sources and every exported proof were reread after rebuilding.

Four-ace approval is pending after this revision. The next art group is the card back, only after approval. No new deck asset has been installed in the game; the completed deck will be installed as one set.
