# Muerta Poker — six-sample audition

6 of six authorized samples are ready: five genuine ElevenLabs SFX auditions using existing Free credits, plus an original locally authored 30-second music excerpt. The music uses physical/modal instrument models, not ElevenLabs music or live recordings. No pack, subscription or credit purchase occurred. No audio is integrated into the game.

These SFX were generated on the **Free** plan and are for this private audition. Commercial game audio needs suitable paid generation rights. [Official commercial-use rules](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform).

Untouched provider SFX MP3 exports are in `samples/originals/`. Their edited WAV masters are 48 kHz / 24-bit, decoded from MP3 rather than lossless-source generation. The shorter spin and stop requests were generated at the API minimum of 0.5 seconds, then trimmed to the approved runtime length. Foreground files use mono. The original local music source is a 48-kHz stereo float32 WAV; its audition master is a 48-kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV.

Only leading silence is removed, with a short end fade. The contacts use fixed gain; the three reward/bonus cues use gain and a 192-kHz oversampled 1-ms look-ahead limiter to reach the approved LUFS and true-peak targets. Exact lengths and meters were verified after editing. Level meters do not certify the musical key, tempo, D-A-C-D motif, timbre or subjective quality.

The five returned SFX billing headers total 40. The immediate end-of-run account balance decreased by 10; these measures differ, so the immediate balance delta is not treated as a final cost. No purchase or billing change occurred.
