# Phase 2B — Queen A court group

All 12 courts are prepared as a single renderer-independent group. **Pending final group approval after the requested K♥ / K♦ neck correction.** No deck or other art has been installed in the game. No aces or later art groups have been generated.

QH is the approved Queen A, copied byte-for-byte at source, vector-card master and both native sizes. Each of the other 11 illustrations was generated separately with Queen A as its direct style reference using the built-in image generator. J/Q/K rank outlines, suit pips and mirrored indices are authored SVG paths. All cards use the approved material, common deterministic crop and exact 180° mirror.

The user requested removal of extra neck skeleton bones in KH and KD. Only those two inputs were revised: one central cervical spine and restrained collarbone ends replace the extra lateral columns. KH additionally received a transparency repair after the neck edit left detached dark background patches. The previous variants, exact edit prompts and source hashes are preserved in `revision-record.json`; ten other courts and the QH anchor were retained.

Review [native HTML proof](native-size-proof.html) at 100% zoom, [all 12 at 86×121](courts-desktop-native.png), and [all 12 at 60×85](courts-portrait-native.png). The HTML uses fixed CSS sizes; PNG proof cards are exported at their actual dimensions, with no zoomed art needed for approval. Original illustrations, vector masters, 512×720 masters and both native exports are included. Every saved file was re-read; the selected asset manifest records sizes and integrity hashes.

Next art group, only after approval: four aces with vector suits. Then card back, frame, backgrounds, bunting, candles and plaque, each approved at final size in that order. The final deck is installed together as one set.
